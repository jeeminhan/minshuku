# Contract 005 — Seeded demo learner

Backlog item: A demo profile with realistic SRS history and 3-4 days of story already progressed, so the interview shows "here's day 5 and how it knew what to teach today." Seed script, deterministic, documented in README.

## Design decisions baked into this contract (read before building)

The MEXT interview must open on a learner who is already days deep — story-so-far on screen, evolved SRS state, today's episode visibly cast from it — without playing those days live on stage. Four decisions resolve how; do not relitigate them:

1. **Seed depth: day 4 (3 days already progressed).** The seed script leaves the learner at the start of day 4 with days 1–3 completed. Why 4 and not 3 or 5: at day 4 the state shows every SRS behavior the demo needs — two items resting at different horizons (`grammar.tsumori` until 06-05, `vocab.ame` until 06-06), one item overdue and rolling forward (`vocab.mado`, due 06-02, unscheduled for two days), one item bouncing back after a 1-day interval (`vocab.fushigi`) — and the day-4 **debrief's due-tomorrow contains `grammar.tsumori` returning** after its 4-day rest, which is the single best "the SRS knew" beat available. Day 3 forfeits that beat and gives only a 2-line story. Day 5 adds little (tsumori as an active) but costs a third new fixture and a 2-active day-5 plan; day 4 needs exactly two new fixtures (day 3 to simulate through, day 4 to play on stage).

2. **The seeded state is ENGINE-COMPUTED, never hand-typed.** The seed script (`cd web && npm run seed-demo`) starts from `freshStoryState()` and simulates days 1–3 through the SAME modules the API routes call — `runEpisode()` (fixture replay, real `runScene`/evaluator) and `completeEpisode()` (real `applyOutcome`/`pickDueItems`) — then writes `web/.data/story-state.json` via `writeStoryState()`. No SRS number in the seed script is a literal: the script must not import `applyOutcome` or contain any `2026-06-*` date string (C3 greps both). Re-running the script is the documented "reset to day 4". The script forces fixture mode internally (`MINSHUKU_FAKE_LLM=1` regardless of environment — it must never call live Gemini, even with `GEMINI_API_KEY` set) and never depends on the pre-existing state file (it rebuilds from fresh — that is what makes C2's byte-identity possible).

3. **Seed enlargement: 7 items — the base 5 (order preserved) plus `grammar.temo-ii` and `vocab.motsu`, appended fresh at the day-4 boundary.** Rationale: (a) appending them any earlier changes the committed days — a fresh grammar item would be the only grammar due on day 2 and steal `pickActiveTargets`' grammar slot, invalidating the committed day-2 fixture and contract-004's pins; (b) appended fresh (all-null SRS fields) they carry no history, so nothing about them is hand-typed — they are "today's new lesson batch", which is how real decks grow; (c) they are the two remaining hand-authored demo items in `data/` with template compatibility (`grammar:てもいい`) and rule-evaluator support (`grammar.dakara` is rejected: its pattern `から` is near-impossible to keep out of NPC lines, which would scaffold every production); (d) they give the seeded day a 2-active plan — a NEW grammar point cast alongside the SRS-due review item `vocab.yakusoku` — while keeping fixture authoring bounded (2 actives + 2 passives). A larger JLPT-sourced expansion adds fixture-authoring surface (every passive needs its surface verbatim in NPC dialogue) with no additional demo-visible signal.

4. **Mandated aggregates for the new fixtures (downstream pins depend on them exactly):** day 3 — `vocab.fushigi → produced` (produced unprompted in EXACTLY one player turn; two clean productions would aggregate to `mastered` per `runScene.aggregateOutcomes` and push fushigi out to 06-07, breaking every day-4 pin below). Day 4 — `grammar.temo-ii → produced` (exactly one clean production) and `vocab.yakusoku → mastered` (produced unprompted in exactly two turns). The builder must verify each aggregate against the real evaluator and document the per-turn outcomes in `generator-state.md`.
