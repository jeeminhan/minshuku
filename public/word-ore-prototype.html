Wired vs. static

One inhabitant talks to a real LLM and plays real TTS audio. The other 18 use stitched placeholder dialogue so you can compare the two.

The android premise

Every inhabitant in this universe is a synthetic intelligence — an old galley unit, a station AI, a salvager drone, a ceremonial android. The "ghosts" you find in derelicts are abandoned operating systems that learned to keep talking. By the time the campaign reveals that Yua on your ship is also one, the player has spent fifteen sessions speaking with her kind. The reveal becomes "of course," not betrayal.
